## Required prose correction and clarifications

**1000 m cutoff has two different roles.** `r8_model.py:55–60,78,89` filters forecast observations by distance for the policy and nominal manager. But `r8_model.py:80–82` computes safety margins for EVERY valid target in the initial observation list over the whole rollout, even if it later leaves 1000 m. The manuscript phrase “retaining the 1000 m observation limit” must be qualified. Suggested text: “Forecast policy and relay observations retain the 1000 m sensing cutoff; clearance scoring continues to extrapolate every initially valid observed target.” New unobserved arrivals are not introduced. Actual evaluation uses all truth targets (`run_r8.py:58–60`). Therefore prediction and real margin comparison must explicitly restrict to a common target set, or flag unmatched targets.

**Midpoint language.** Manuscript III-B says “actual midpoint sail/rudder angles.” Code uses arithmetic means of stored endpoint angles, not independently observed continuous mid-step angles (`r8_model.py:35,40`). Write “midpoint averages of observed endpoint sail/rudder angles.” Heading uses shortest-angle interpolation; roll does not.

**Fallback wind language.** With fewer than three pairs, code retains `self.wind`, which is the prior initially or the previous estimate subsequently, not necessarily the prior (`r8_model.py:31–32`). This cannot normally occur after eligibility at t>=10 with full history, but the precise generic wording is “retains the current estimate.”

**No inferred accuracy proof.** “Each branch restores Xi_t” is acceptable shorthand after explaining reconstruction and the base-after-nominal ordering. Do not replace it with an unsupported assertion that all hidden tensors were tested equal. First-action equality is a necessary consistency check only.
