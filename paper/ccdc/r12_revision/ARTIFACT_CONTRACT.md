# R12 document contract

The editable source is manuscript.docx, produced from manuscript.md by build_docx.py using the user-supplied conference-template.docx (retained as official_template.docx). Body and section styles are inherited. Equations are editable Office Math generated from explicit MathML in math_docx.py, in the same order as the five displayed equations in Markdown. They are not raster images. Microsoft Word exports the final PDF. Direct Word edits do not round-trip to Markdown automatically.

All figures are derived from recorded simulated physical trajectories, predictions, or task records except Figure 1, which is an explicitly schematic execution-interface diagram inherited from R11. Results are kept separate by evidence group. Detailed proofs and reproducibility notes are in SUPPLEMENT.md. Unknown author identities, inherited-asset provenance and permissions are centralized in AUTHOR_ASSET_CHECKLIST_ZH.md.

The template establishes layout, not independent confirmation of every current submission policy. The prior official-site policy check is retained; unresolved final-page, anonymity and disclosure details remain author actions. No submission was performed.
